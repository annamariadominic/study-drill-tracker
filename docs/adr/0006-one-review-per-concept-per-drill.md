# A Drill counts as one review per Concept, signalled by recall

Drill composition can ask about the same Concept more than once — a weak Concept gets both a recall and a flashcard Question, and a Concept may additionally appear inside a scenario Question — so letting every Attempt advance the review schedule applied SM-2's interval multiplier several times in one sitting. That pushed the weakest Concepts' next review furthest out, the opposite of what drilling them harder is for: a newly-studied Concept answered well twice jumped to a 9-day interval, where a stronger Concept asked once moved 6 days to 17.

A Drill therefore advances each Concept's schedule **at most once**. Which Attempt provides that update is decided by Question type rather than by order: where the Drill asks a recall Question about the Concept, the first recall Attempt is the signal, since producing the answer is stricter evidence than recognising it among options or meeting the Concept inside a wider scenario. Where the Drill asks no recall Question about that Concept — a strong Concept given only a flashcard, or a Concept that appears only inside a scenario — the first Attempt on whatever it does ask provides the one update. Attempts that aren't the signal are still recorded and graded in full; they simply don't move the schedule.

We considered aggregating a Drill's Attempts and applying a single update at the end, but a Drill abandoned half way would then advance nothing. We also considered "first Attempt wins" regardless of type, which is simpler but lets a flashcard or scenario Attempt pre-empt the recall evidence when it happens to come first in the sequence. The cost of preferring recall is that abandoning a Drill before reaching a Concept's recall Question leaves that Concept unadvanced even if a flashcard on it was answered; we accept that, because the recall Attempt is the evidence the schedule is meant to reflect.

This refines ADR 0002 rather than replacing it: Correctness and Confidence still drive the interval and ease factor exactly as before, and reviews outside a Drill still advance on every Attempt. It is hard to reverse for the same reason ADR 0002 is — accumulated review state is computed under this rule, and changing it later would misread every interval already stored.
